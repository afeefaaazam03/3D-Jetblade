Does the evidence test reduce false damage claims while keeping recall?

Does it depend on blade size?

  • Separation (AUROC): 0.74 on CAD6 vs 0.82 on the Rotor37 sealed test (transfer gap 0.08).
  • Visible dents found 94%, false alarms 67%, hidden dents abstained 33%.
  • Best blade 1 (24 mm), worst blade 3 (31 mm).
  • False alarms by blade, smallest to largest: 100%, 100%, 100%, 82%, 6%, 15%; Spearman ρ(blade size, false alarms) = -0.88 (p = 0.02).
  • Blade size vs. dents found, across 6 blades: Spearman ρ = -0.93 (p = 0.01; only 6 blades, so weak evidence).
  • Smaller blades (≤ median span) found 99% vs larger 88%. The same dent in mm is relatively larger on a small blade, so we also check depth relative to the blade: ρ(found, relative depth) = 0.10, ρ(found, depth in mm) = 0.02.
  • Found rate by relative-depth tertile (shallow → deep): 92%, 92%, 97%.
  • With a threshold recalibrated leave-one-blade-out on CAD6 negatives: found 13%, false alarms 4%.
  • Recommended blade for the physical capture: blade 5 (better transfer of blades 4 and 5).
