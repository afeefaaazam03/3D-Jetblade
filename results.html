Does the evidence test reduce false damage claims while keeping recall?

Without it, the same fits would raise false alarms on 32% of healthy/stained blades and wrongly "find" 55% of hidden dents. With it: 10% and 15%. Recall on visible dents drops from 84% to 54%. Detected dents: median depth error 0.030 mm, centre error 0.13 mm.
